Company

The company on record.

Texas-registered and US-based.

AllSet Supply LLC is a Texas limited liability company — a US-based product distribution and sourcing operation, owner-operated end to end. When you contact AllSet Supply, you are dealing with the owner, not a hand-off.

We sell primarily through our Amazon storefront — alongside other marketplace and retail channels, and we run brokered procurement for commercial and wholesale buyers — purchase orders, one-time volume buys, and recurring supply programs.

Fulfillment runs from two US locations: self-fulfillment out of Houston, Texas, and large-order B2B fulfillment out of Vandergrift, Pennsylvania through a fulfillment partner network. Product experience runs through consumer electronics, connectivity and communications hardware, and tools and equipment — brand-name lines like Ryobi, RIDGID, Square D, and Eaton.
